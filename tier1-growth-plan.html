Create the
core assets

1
Tier 1 proposition one-pager
A simple articulation of why Jungle is relevant to large consumer brands now. Core message: Jungle helps ambitious brands turn social from a content channel into a commercial growth system.
2
Intermediary deck
10–12 slides that make it easy for intermediaries to know when to put Jungle on the list. Must include what we do, who we're perfect for, what problems we solve, 2–3 case studies, a clear ICP slide, and a "when to call us" slide.
3
Tier 1 outreach narrative
A short POV-led message sequence avoiding cold sales language. Key themes: social has moved into the boardroom; vanity metrics are no longer enough; large brands need connected social systems, not isolated content.
4
Hero case studies
Priority: DFS, Disney, Red Bull, Itsu, Optimum Nutrition (if usable). Each must prove commercial relevance, scale, social maturity and connected delivery.
5
Thought leadership hook
Core POV: "Vanity is Dead: why social now needs to prove commercial impact." This becomes the engine for every outreach, event invite and follow-up.

Account-based
pursuit

For each Tier A account, run a 7-step engagement sequence. Account plans to cover: why it matters, likely business issue, Jungle relevance, best route in, outreach angle, and named owner.

01
Research
Identify social maturity gap, current brand activity, business triggers and likely pain points.
02
Map people
Find 3–5 relevant contacts per account across marketing, brand, social, content and media.
03
Identify best route in
Warm intro first. Intermediary second. Direct outreach third.
04
Send tailored outreach
A short POV-led message — not a generic sales email.
05
Invite to useful engagement
Roundtable, briefing, private session or tailored conversation.
06
Nurture
Continue with relevant content, case studies, category observations and event follow-ups.
07
Convert to meeting
Aim for a strategic conversation — not a credentials presentation.
