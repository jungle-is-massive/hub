The case for HubSpot
Why this isn't just an email tool

HubSpot at cost is only justified if it's the engine — not a mailing list. Here's what we're actually paying for when we use it properly.

🎯
Pipeline visibility across two teams
Agency Pipeline + Partnerships Pipeline
  • Every live deal tracked with stage, value, owner and next action
  • Both teams have shared line of sight on what's active vs. stale
  • Deal data surfaces in the BD Hub dashboard in real-time
⚡
Contact lifecycle + nurture automation
MQL to SQL to Deal
  • Contacts move through lifecycle stages automatically based on behaviour
  • Email sequences nurture MQLs without manual send-by-send effort
  • BCC email tracking keeps every conversation logged without effort
🌐
Landing pages & gated content
Events, downloads, lead gen
  • Build event RSVP pages without dev resource — fully tracked in CRM
  • Gate playbooks, reports and toolkits behind forms that auto-create contacts
  • Every form submission becomes an enriched HubSpot contact with source tracking
📊
Reporting that shows what's working
Revenue attribution
  • Source reporting: which channel (LinkedIn, event, referral, inbound) is converting
  • Pipeline velocity: how fast deals move from MQL to close
  • Win rate, deal size, and cycle time by team and by pipeline
